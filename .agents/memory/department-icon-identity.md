---
name: Department icon identity
description: How department symbols should carry through objective surfaces without obscuring status.
---

Use the same department symbol on department and objective surfaces. For an objective, derive the department from its responsible manager; use a neutral fallback if no department can be resolved. Keep objective status in its own label or badge rather than changing the identity symbol by status.

**Why:** The user asked for improved department icons and specifically wanted objectives to use the department icons too. A stable symbol makes related work recognizable across roles and pages.

**How to apply:** Reuse the central department-symbol mapping on new department or objective UI. Preserve an explicit status indicator alongside objective identity.
