---
name: Department home trend
description: How the Superadmin home card should explain department direction.
---

The Superadmin department preview uses the number of tasks actually completed from Monday through now against the same elapsed interval last week. Show improvement as a green up arrow with an "Up" label, decline as a red down arrow with a "Down" label, and unchanged counts neutrally. Keep the card to four ranked departments.

**Why:** Due-date-only completion rates misclassified Engineering as declining even though it had completed more tasks this week than in the equivalent portion of last week. The user expects Engineering's increased completed work to show as improving.

**How to apply:** For this home card, compare completion timestamps and show both the current completed count and last week's count. Don't imply a rate is being shown when the metric is a task count.
