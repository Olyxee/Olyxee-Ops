Update the intern management system so that any intern currently assigned to an **Unknown**, **Unassigned**, empty, null, or invalid department is automatically reassigned to the most relevant valid department based on their role, title, skills, and current responsibilities.

Use these official departments:

1. **Research & Development (R&D)**

   * Software Engineering
   * Full-Stack Engineering
   * Frontend Engineering
   * Backend Engineering
   * Product Engineering
   * Research Engineering
   * Technical Research
   * Prototyping and experimentation

2. **AI Engineering**

   * AI Engineering
   * Machine Learning Engineering
   * LLM / Agent Engineering
   * Applied AI
   * AI integrations
   * Model development
   * MLOps where primarily AI-focused

3. **Data & Security**

   * Data Engineering
   * Data Science
   * ETL / Data Pipelines
   * Databases
   * Data Governance
   * Cybersecurity
   * Security Engineering
   * Access Control
   * Data Protection

4. **Sales & Marketing**

   * Sales
   * Marketing
   * Business Development
   * Partnerships
   * Customer Success
   * Communications

5. **Business Operations**

   * Operations
   * Finance
   * HR / People
   * Administration
   * Legal / Compliance
   * Project coordination

### Reassignment logic

For every existing intern:

* If their current department is `Unknown`, `Unassigned`, blank, null, missing, deprecated, or does not match one of the official departments above, determine the correct department from their role/title.
* Preserve interns who already have a valid department unless their department is clearly inconsistent with their role.
* Do not delete or duplicate any intern records.
* Do not modify unrelated intern information such as name, email, start date, end date, projects, notes, or account permissions.

Examples:

* `AI Engineering Intern` → **AI Engineering**
* `Machine Learning Intern` → **AI Engineering**
* `Data Engineering Intern` → **Data & Security**
* `Cybersecurity Intern` → **Data & Security**
* `Software Engineering Intern` → **Research & Development (R&D)**
* `Frontend Intern` → **Research & Development (R&D)**
* `Backend Intern` → **Research & Development (R&D)**
* `Research Engineer Intern` → **Research & Development (R&D)**
* `Marketing Intern` → **Sales & Marketing**
* `Business Development Intern` → **Sales & Marketing**
* `Operations Intern` → **Business Operations**

### Implementation requirements

Create a reusable department-classification function rather than hardcoding individual intern names.

Use role/title keyword matching with normalized lowercase text. Make the mapping easy to extend later.

If an intern's role cannot be confidently mapped, keep them in an explicit **Unassigned** state and flag them for Supaadmin review instead of guessing.

Update both:

* existing database records where appropriate;
* the UI so no intern is displayed under an `Unknown Department` section when their role can clearly determine the department.

Add a migration/backfill script for existing intern records.

The migration must:

* be safe to run more than once;
* not create duplicates;
* log which interns were changed;
* record the previous department and new department;
* skip records that do not require changes.

After migration, verify that every active intern belongs to one of the five official departments or is explicitly marked `Unassigned`.

Also update department dropdowns, filters, profile views, forms, and dashboards so they only use these official department names consistently throughout the application.
